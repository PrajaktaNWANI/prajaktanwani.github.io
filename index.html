How I run a release

  1. Plan the release with the client and the teams involved.
  2. Support User Acceptance Testing (UAT) and resolve defects as they appear.
  3. Coordinate development, support and DevOps.
  4. Prepare a rollback plan with a time estimate and steps.
  5. Support go-live and verify the release in production.

Complex releases run about two days. Sitefinity platform upgrades are among them.

About

I have 8+ years in IT, including 2 years leading a 6-member Agile team and onsite client delivery in the UK from March 2023 to June 2025. At Infosys, I have worked on the ABB account since 2022, on both new requirements and maintenance, with new features and upgrades shipping twice a month.

Process improvements raised sprint velocity by 20%, with 100% sprint completion. On the analysis side, I support Business Analysts with requirements, Business Requirements Documents (BRDs), process flows and acceptance criteria, take part in UAT and write functional and technical documentation for delivered tools. I have trained backend developers to take on frontend tasks and review all frontend pull requests, through a review process that controls release risk before changes reach production. As sole UI owner on several ABB tools, I also handled communication with the ABB business units that requested them, from first request to release.
